RESIDENTIAL RENT AGREEMENT
This agreement is made between Mr. Ravi Kumar (Landlord) and Ms. Anita Rao (Tenant).
1. Term: 11 months starting 1 October 2026.
2. Rent: Rs. 15,000 per month, payable by the 5th of each month. Late payment attracts a penalty of Rs. 500 per day.
3. Security deposit: Rs. 90,000, refundable within 90 days after vacating, after deductions decided solely by the Landlord.
4. Termination: The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time without notice. The Tenant must give 3 months' notice.
5. Maintenance: The Tenant bears all repair costs, including structural repairs.
6. Rent may be increased by the Landlord at any time without consent.
