LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made between Maya Patel (Landlord) and Arjun Shah (Tenant) for the property located at 12 River Street, Bengaluru.

1. Term
The tenancy shall commence on 1st January 2026 and continue for a period of 12 months unless terminated earlier by either party in accordance with this Agreement.

2. Rent
The Tenant shall pay rent of INR 32,000 per month, due on the 5th day of each month. Payment must be made by bank transfer to the Landlord's designated account.

3. Security Deposit
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of INR 96,000 before taking possession of the premises. The deposit shall be refunded within 30 days after lease termination, subject to any unpaid rent or damages.

4. Late Payment
If rent is not paid within 7 days of the due date, a late fee of 10% of the monthly rent shall apply. The Landlord may also serve a notice to cure and terminate the lease if default continues.

5. Maintenance and Repairs
The Tenant shall keep the premises in good condition and is responsible for day-to-day maintenance. Structural repairs and major maintenance remain the responsibility of the Landlord.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this lease upon 30 days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement. The Tenant may terminate early only if the Landlord fails to provide the premises in a habitable condition.

7. Use of Premises
The property shall be used solely for residential purposes. The Tenant shall not sublet or assign the lease without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

8. Inspection
The Landlord may inspect the property with 24 hours' prior notice for maintenance or compliance checks.

9. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Karnataka, India.

10. Notice
All notices must be sent in writing by registered post or email to the addresses set out in this Agreement.

Signatures:
Landlord: Maya Patel
Tenant: Arjun Shah
